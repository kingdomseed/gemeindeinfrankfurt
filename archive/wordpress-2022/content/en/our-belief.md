# Our Belief

#### We hold the faith which is common to all believers (Titus 1:4, Jude 3)

---

**THE BIBLE**Our belief is based on the Bible, which is the complete divine revelation inspired, word by word, by God through the Holy Spirit.  
(2 Pet. 1:21, 2 Tim. 3:16)

**THE TRIUNE GOD**God is uniquely one, yet a Trinity—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.  
(1 Tim. 2:5, Matt. 28:19)

**JESUS CHRIST**The Son of God, even God Himself, was incarnated to be a man by the name of Jesus Christ.  
(John 1:1, 14)

**CHRIST’S CRUCIFIXION**Christ died on the cross for our sins, shedding His blood for our redemption.  
(1 Pet. 2:24, Eph. 1:7)

**CHRIST’S RESURRECTION**Christ resurrected from among the dead on the third day.  
(1 Cor. 15:4)

**CHRIST’S ASCENSION**Christ ascended to the right hand of God to be Lord of all.  
(Acts 1:9, 2:33, 2:36)

**REGENERATION**Whenever anyone repents to God and believes in the Lord Jesus Christ, he or she is regenerated (born again) and becomes a living member of the one Body of Christ.  
(Acts 20:21, John 3:3, Eph. 1:22-23, Rom. 12:5)

**CHRIST’S RETURN**Christ is coming again to receive His believers to Himself.  
(1 Thes. 2:19)
