# Domain Glossary

Shared terminology for the church in Frankfurt's video training platform.

## Training & Schedule

- **Training**: One local video training cycle with its title, schedule, fee, outline price, bank payment details, venue, localities, attendance locations, services, and post-training report.
- **Message / Session**: One scheduled video message in a training, numbered sequentially (typically 1–12) and stored in Frankfurt time (`Europe/Berlin`).
- **Format**: Weekday messages (including Friday) are played together online using the private Zoom link shared after registration. Weekend messages (Saturday and Lord's Day) are attended in person at one of the training's attendance locations.

## Registration & Seats

- **Registration**: A registrant's sign-up for one seat in a training, managed without an account through a private personal link.
- **Seat**: The participation mode chosen on registration:
  - **Full-time**: Attends all messages in the training.
  - **Part-time**: Chooses specific messages in advance; paid the same donation as full-time.
  - **Shared seat**: Two people sharing one full-time seat (such as spouses caring for a child at home) under one donation, with one person attending each message.
  - **Live training (Anaheim) seat**: For saints attending the live training in Anaheim who also wish to join local video sessions; owes no donation and receives no outline.
- **Outline**: The mandatory printed training outline required for every non-Anaheim seat, plus an optional second outline in another language (German, English, or Russian). Outlines are invoiced separately from the seat donation.
- **Locality**: The registrant's home city: either Frankfurt am Main (the home locality) or a neighboring city registering through Frankfurt and included in Frankfurt's report to Lebensstrom.
- **Attendance location (Site)**: A physical gathering place where weekend video sessions are played in person (for example, Frankfurt am Main or Mannheim/Heidelberg).
- **Edit link**: A unique personal URL included in confirmation emails that allows viewing, editing, or cancelling one registration without a login. Issuing a new edit link invalidates the previous one.
- **Cutoffs**: Automatic deadlines calculated from the schedule in Frankfurt time. New registrations close at the end of the first training day; seat type changes, part-time session selection changes, and self-service cancellation lock when the first message starts. Name, contact details, languages, and comments stay editable by the registrant throughout the training; coordinators may edit or cancel after any cutoff.

## Payments

- **Donation**: The per-seat training fee (set per training, transferred manually by bank transfer to the church account) and separate outline amount, tracked by hand as paid or unpaid.
- **Hardship waiver**: A coordinator-granted waiver of the seat donation with a private reason kept for coordinators and never shown to the registrant. Outlines are still paid unless the seat is an Anaheim seat.

## Coordination

- **Coordinator**: A serving saint authorized to sign in to the shared live coordination workspace. Any coordinator can view and manage registrations, payments, outlines, setup, study groups, attendance, messages, emails, and reporting.
- **AV coordinator**: A coordinator serving in AV (Technik), the only role permitted to view or update Lebensstrom webcast credentials.
- **Study group**: A small language- and locality-based group (typically 8–10 trainees) used for mutual sharing and testing. Late registrants automatically join the smallest existing group matching their study-group language.
- **Testing**: Scheduled group sharing/recitation placed before chosen messages (typically messages 1–10).
- **Service (Aufgabe)**: A practical training responsibility assigned to a coordinator lead (such as Tech, Rules, Hymns, Verses, Questions, Attendance, Payments, Name tags, or Groups), plus any custom coordinator-added services.
- **Attendance mark & Absence limit**: A per-session status of present, absent, or excused (or unmarked). Over-limit means any unexcused absence or more than two excused absences across sessions required for that seat; this count is for the post-training report only.
- **Webcast access vs Local Zoom link**: Webcast access is the Lebensstrom login for the LSM playback website, restricted strictly to AV coordinators and never shared with registrants. The local Zoom link for weekday online sessions is sent privately to registered saints through the WhatsApp group and never posted publicly.
- **Lebensstrom report & Reimbursements**: The post-training summary (seat counts, donations collected, outline revenue, waived Ukrainian saints, dates, questionnaire answers, per-message absences, and reimbursable expenses with invoice photos) due within 7 days after the last message.
